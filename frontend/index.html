How Sentiment Analysis AI Works (Beginner Guide)

🧠

1. What is Natural Language Processing?

NLP is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables computers to understand, interpret, and generate human language. Sentiment analysis decodes the subjective emotional state behind words.

📐

2. The VADER Algorithm

VADER (Valence Aware Dictionary and sEntiment Reasoner) uses a curated lexicon of words mapped to intensity ratings, combined with 5 grammatical heuristic rules (capitalization, punctuation, booster words, negations).

⚡

3. How Scores are Calculated

Scores are normalized into a Compound Score between -1.0 (extreme negative) and +1.0 (extreme positive). Sentences with scores $\ge +0.05$ are categorized as Positive, while $\le -0.05$ are Negative.

🚀

4. Modern Transformers vs Lexicons

While Lexicon analyzers like VADER provide instant, lightweight zero-latency analysis, Deep Learning Transformer models (like BERT and RoBERTa) use multi-head self-attention to understand context and sarcasm.

SentimAI Quick Guide

Welcome to SentimAI Studio! Here is how to use and learn from this project:

  • Real-time Analysis: Type any text into the box to see live sentiment updates as you type.
  • Explore Presets: Click on preset tags (e.g. 5-Star Review, Support Ticket) to test real-world scenarios.
  • Token Highlighting: Look at the inspector box to see which specific words contributed positive (green) or negative (red) polarity.
  • Batch Analysis: Switch to the Batch tab to test multiple reviews at once or upload CSV datasets.
